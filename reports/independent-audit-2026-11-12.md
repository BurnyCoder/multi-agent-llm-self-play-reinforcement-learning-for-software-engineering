# Independent factual audit and retrospective corrections

Date: 2026-11-12. Scope: this checkout, the saved pilot attempts, the existing publication bundle, and their public GitHub Pages and Hugging Face surfaces. Previous audit conclusions were not used as evidence. This is a retrospective correction, not a preregistration amendment or a new experiment. Original preregistration, checkpoints, logs, model outputs and upstream notices are preserved.

Numerical observations are in the generated [raw-record cross-check][audit-data], [report summary][summary], and [source verification][sources]. They are calculated from files, not transcribed into this ledger. `selfplay report --run runs/pilot-recovery` regenerates empirical tables, figures, paper, dashboard, and the independent raw-record cross-check. Tests and publication checks are recorded separately in [verification][verification].

**Reading the ledger:** “verified” means the stated narrow claim is supported by the identified evidence. It does not certify the whole system. “corrected” identifies a repaired factual or semantic error. “qualified” preserves an observation while limiting its interpretation. “unknown” marks a claim the available evidence cannot establish. Requirements and design settings are not observations. Repeated generated copies share the canonical claim entry below; upstream notices and historical model outputs are not newly authored factual assertions.

The primary papers were read beyond their abstracts, including the method equations and appendices relevant to this implementation. Source revisions and retrieved-byte hashes are recorded in the source verification artifact. Link accessibility is a separate check from citation support. An inaccessible source, absent log, or unexecuted intervention is not counted as verification.

This ledger covers the substantive project-authored claim families and their generated repetitions. The [file coverage inventory][coverage] maps each tracked surface to these entries. It explicitly classifies upstream notices, fixtures, frozen protocol text, and historical outputs. This audit does not claim universal error-freedom, full benchmark validity, unrestricted dataset licensing, new experimental evidence, or causal identification.
